WISE Platform — Progress Update
August 13–14, 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Over these two days the team delivered a major upgrade to the WISEcodeAI
experience, introduced a workspace organization system across the entire
platform, and completed a product-wide design-consistency pass. The result is
an app that feels more like one coherent product: every page now shares the
same AI chat, the same layout behaviors, and the same visual language in both
light and dark mode.

HIGHLIGHTS

1. Smarter, more transparent AI workspace
   The WISEcodeAI workspace now treats AI outputs like living documents.
   Every chart, report, or table the AI produces is time-stamped and
   versioned — ask the AI to redo its work and the previous versions stay
   visible in an elegant stacked history, so users can see exactly how an
   answer improved (demonstrated end-to-end with a recipe-optimization story
   whose score climbs from Good to Excellent across three versions). Outputs
   can be expanded directly inside the conversation, browsed from a new
   dropdown menu, and the AI now visibly "thinks out loud" before presenting
   results, building user trust in how answers are produced.

2. A tidier, more flexible workspace on every page
   A new "sticky module" behavior was rolled out platform-wide: any panel
   sitting beside the AI chat can now tuck in behind it like a drawer,
   keeping the screen calm and focused while remaining one click away. Panel
   widths were standardized across the whole product, and the chat now
   respects the reader — it never yanks the screen away while a long answer
   is arriving.

3. AI Operations Dashboard, fully integrated
   The dashboard for monitoring AI usage, spend, and guardrails was rebuilt
   from a standalone page into the standard product shell, complete with its
   own AI assistant that can answer questions about usage and cost directly
   from the dashboard data. All charts were redrawn in the WISE brand
   palette so the page reads as part of the family.

4. Conversation Library: folders arrive
   Users can now organize their saved reports, dashboards, and chats into
   color-coded folders — drag a card onto a folder to file it, or drop one
   card onto another to start a new folder on the spot. Folders can be
   renamed, recolored, and dissolved, and the library's filters were refined
   so the numbers on screen always match what users see.

5. Product Portfolio: from browsing to doing
   The portfolio's quick actions now launch real guided workflows: verifying
   ingredients opens the live Verification flow, activating Non-UPF Shields
   opens the SHIELD workspace, and reformulation opportunities open the
   Reformulation Plan — turning the portfolio from a list into a launchpad.

6. Personalization and accessibility
   Users can now upload a profile picture or pick from ready-made avatar
   patterns, and their avatar follows them into every AI conversation.
   Separately, the platform's built-in accessibility audit now grades the
   app continuously, and that grade is surfaced in the appearance settings —
   green, amber, or red — so accessibility stays visible, not buried.

7. Design consistency, light and dark
   A platform-wide color-token migration means brand-blue text now renders
   correctly on every page in dark mode. Combined with the standardized
   layouts and shared components, virtually every page in the product was
   touched and brought in line.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BUSINESS
- Demo-ready polish: the AI workspace now tells a compelling, visual story
  of iteration and improvement — ideal for customer demonstrations.
- One product, not many pages: consistent behavior and branding across the
  entire platform reduces user confusion and support friction.
- Trust and transparency: visible AI reasoning, versioned outputs, and a
  live accessibility grade all reinforce credibility with buyers.
